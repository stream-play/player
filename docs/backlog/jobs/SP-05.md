## States & edge cases
SP-05 empty state: `Geen stream: player error card met retry, andere feed en rapportage.`. On loading, reserve the final layout; on local error, offer Retry while other content remains usable; offline and partial data must be distinguished. Restore keyboard focus after dialogs and honor reduced motion. Gate startover, pause and feed switching on real capabilities; report DRM or stream failure locally.
